Undaergo — routing data NOTICE
==============================

The routing archive distributed beside the installer, `routing-italy-3.9.0.tar.gz`, holds a
routing graph and the engine's command-line tools. The operator imports it from the Data
panel; the graph is not inside the installer.

  1. A routing graph tiled by Valhalla from a Geofabrik OpenStreetMap extract of Italy.
  2. The compiled Valhalla tools, redistributed with the application as well.

Engine
------

  Version:  3.9.0
  Licence:  MIT
  Source:   https://github.com/valhalla/valhalla
  Binaries: unpacked from the "pyvalhalla" distribution published by the project on PyPI.
            Only the compiled executables and their shared libraries are redistributed; no

  Licence file: LICENSE-Valhalla-MIT.txt

The engine loads third-party shared libraries, each under its own licence. They are listed,
with their copyright notices and upstream addresses, in NOTICE-bundle.txt, which must travel
with this notice.

Routing graph
-------------

  Publisher: OpenStreetMap contributors
  Extract:   Geofabrik regional extract of Italy
  Licence:   Open Database License, version 1.0 (ODbL 1.0)
  Copyright: © OpenStreetMap contributors
             https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

  Licence file: LICENSE-ODbL-1.0.txt

The graph is a Derivative Database of OpenStreetMap data under the ODbL, so the attribution
obligation is in force at the moment of shipping, not on use. The credit is carried on the map
itself, declared on the "italy" source in the application's map style and shown as
"© OpenStreetMap contributors" — the same string the basemap displays, because both derive
from OpenStreetMap.

The ODbL requires that anyone receiving the graph also receives the licence: the text is in
this folder as LICENSE-ODbL-1.0.txt, and the operator may redistribute the graph only under
that licence.

The exact extract, its digest, the engine version and the tile counts that shipped are
recorded in PROVENANCE.json inside the archive. Read the numbers from that file rather than
restating them here.
